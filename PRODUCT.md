# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Gift senders, event hosts, couples, and celebrants in Panama City looking for high-end balloon decor, fresh flower arrangements, personalized surprise gifts, and event furniture rentals.

## Product Purpose
Ballonsdei Boutique is a luxury floral and balloon gift atelier e-commerce platform. It enables customers to discover curated collections, reserve event furniture, and compose bespoke gift arrangements with a 2D interactive visual studio that produces transparent cost breakdowns and streamlined WhatsApp/Yappy orders.

## Positioning
An interactive visual atelier where customers see and customize their arrangement in real-time—combining balloons, florals, custom vinyl messages, ribbons, and plushies—before dispatching the exact configuration directly to the artisan florist via WhatsApp and Yappy.

## Operating Context
- Geographic focus: Panama City delivery and atelier pickup.
- Currency: USD ($).
- Payment methods: Yappy (Banco General QR & phone), cash on delivery, bank transfer.
- Operational workflows: Customer ordering -> WhatsApp confirmation -> Artisan production tracking -> Dispatch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Interactive 2D visual customizer with transparent cartoon SVG elements (flowers, balloons, toppers, ribbons, chocolates, plush toys).
- Responsive mobile & desktop interface with zero layout shift.
- Cart drawer with gift itemization and custom arrangement design references.
- Administrative control panel for products, categories, orders, production queues, and customizer elements.
- Emil Kowalski tactile physics: 160ms `--ease-out` rebound, 75ms instant press feedback, `@media (hover: hover)` fine-pointer gating.
- Impeccable UI anti-pattern detection and accessibility safeguards.

## Brand Commitments
- Brand name: Ballonsdei Boutique.
- Palette: Royal plum (`#763D8B`), warm champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), soft cream backdrop (`#FAF8F5`), rose and earthy botanical undertones.
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond (refined luxury serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans (clean modern sans-serif).
- Voice: Warm, celebratory, attentive, and artisanal.

## Product Principles
1. **Artisanal Fidelity:** What the customer designs in the studio matches what the atelier crafts in reality.
2. **Effortless Gifting:** Simple, transparent pricing with no hidden fees or friction at checkout.
3. **Tactile Craft:** Every button, card, and modal responds with immediate organic feedback.
4. **Panamanian Familiarity:** Seamless integration with local habits (Yappy, WhatsApp direct dispatch).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict contrast compliance on typography.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` across all animations.
- Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 44x44px).
